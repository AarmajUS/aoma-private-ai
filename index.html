Private AI

An AI chat that runs 100% on your own device. After a one-time model download, nothing you type is sent to any server — not to us, not to anyone. Close the tab and the conversation is gone.

Preparing…

🔒 How the privacy works: the AI model downloads once (from a public model library) and is cached in your browser. Every answer is then generated by your own phone/computer's graphics chip. There is no account, no server copy, no chat log. Honest limits: this is a compact model — sharp for drafts, ideas and everyday questions, not a match for the giant cloud AIs on hard research. It is also fully **offline**: it cannot check today's news, weather or prices — everything it knows came with the download. On a phone, keep this tab on screen while it loads and thinks; switching apps mid-answer can make the browser drop the page. That trade is the price of total privacy, and for a lot of daily work it's worth it.
